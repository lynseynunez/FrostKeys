## What's new in SakuriKey 1.1

**Check for updates now installs new builds right in the app.** Tap it in Settings → About and follow the prompts.

**Looks and settings**
- Looks gallery: 14 built-in looks as thumbnails, plus save your own
- Settings are easier to get around: search, live summaries, recently changed, and Advanced sections
- Frosted glass everywhere: long-press popups (with adjustable background and blur), clipboard, GIFs, translator, AI and text tools
- New glass animations (rain, fireflies, waves) and multi-color key spotlight

**Typing**
- Long-press accents and symbols you can tune: accent languages, order, two-row menus
- Smart paste: a copied email, phone number or link appears in the suggestion bar
- Calculator and unit chips: type 12*8 or 5 km and tap the answer
- Smarter autocorrect with about 80,000 common word pairs

**Keyboard**
- Landscape: shorter keyboard, full width, corner symbols next to their letters
- Width and position options, split keys, and automatic sizing on tablets

**Clipboard and GIFs**
- Clipboard has Recent and Pinned tabs, and you can pin the text in a field
- Favorite GIFs: star a GIF to keep it
- Backups include pinned items and favorite GIFs

**Under the hood**
- Settings are tested automatically before every build, so a crash on opening Settings can't reach your phone
